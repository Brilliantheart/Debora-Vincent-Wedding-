OUR LOVE STORY

Two perspectives. One beautiful story.

Every love story is told from two hearts, two perspectives and two journeys. This is ours — from the moment our paths crossed at work to the moment we chose to walk through life together.

VINCENT'S STORY

She was the one.

Our paths first crossed at DP World Logistics, where we both went about our daily duties, never knowing that destiny had already placed our names on the same page.

From the very first day I saw Deborah, something within me stirred. I could not explain it, but there was a quiet conviction in my heart: “She is the one.”

I did not see her as someone I simply wanted to date. I saw the woman with whom I wanted to build a life, share my dreams, weather life's storms and grow old beside.

The journey, however, was not as easy as I imagined. 😂 There were moments when my hope grew faint and I wondered if the future I had envisioned was only a dream.

But true love has a strange way of refusing to die. And then came that fateful night when we finally spoke openly about our feelings, our fears and what we truly wanted.

“I was not seeking a passing romance. I was seeking her — for a lifetime.”

That conversation changed everything. What began as colleagues became friendship, friendship became affection, affection became love, and love eventually became a promise of forever.

&
DEBORAH'S STORY

From “wake up” to forever.

If someone had told me I would meet the love of my life at work, I probably would have laughed. Because really, who goes to work looking for love? 😂

It all began on the very first day Vincent resumed at my branch. I noticed him looking at me — not once, not twice, but enough times for me to finally ask, “Why do you keep looking at me?”

His response was simple and confident: “Because you are my wife.”

I laughed and told him, “Please, wake up from that dream.” 😂 Little did I know that I was standing at the beginning of the very dream he was talking about.

Vincent was more experienced on the job and was assigned to teach me some aspects of the work I was unfamiliar with. So technically, he entered my life as a colleague and a teacher.

But somewhere along the way, he started teaching me something completely different — what it feels like to be cared for.

He was patient, helpful, protective and consistent. I tried to keep everything strictly professional, but somehow he kept finding his way through every boundary I carefully built. 😂

Eventually, I gave him a chance to talk to me outside work, and that conversation became the beginning of something neither of us could have planned.

The more we talked, the more I discovered how much we had in common. Somewhere between fleet management, daily fuel wahala, work assignments and random conversations, our hearts found each other.

“I prayed about it — and God gave me my green light.” 💚

The woman who once laughed and told a man to wake up from his dream was now standing in the reality of an answered prayer.

FROM WORKPLACE TO FOREVER

We met at work, but fate brought us together.

What began as an ordinary workplace encounter became an extraordinary journey of friendship, love, faith and commitment. Two people who never planned to find love at work found something far greater than they could have imagined.

“I choose thee then. I choose thee now. And I shall choose thee forever.” ❤️
